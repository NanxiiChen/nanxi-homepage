> **Abstract.** Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) have recently emerged as a promising tool for resolving partial differential equations. However, when confronting complex multi-physics problems, conventional PINNs suffer from substantial challenges. In this study, we present an advanced PINN framework tailored for the phase-field model of pitting corrosion (PC-PINNs), effectively navigating the complexities of the rigidly coupled Allen-Cahn and Cahn-Hilliard equations. The main ingredients of PC-PINNs include: 1) an NTK-based adaptive weighting strategy to balance the multiple loss terms arising from coupled equations, 2) a generalized adaptive sampling scheme complemented by dual criteria to accurately track the corrosion interface, 3) a random-batch technique to accelerate the weight calculation, and 4) a cross-scale sampling technique to ensure the veracity of convergence. We evaluate the PC-PINNs against two phase-field corrosion benchmarks, using various strategy permutations to validate the effectiveness of the proposed theory. Results demonstrate that the PC-PINNs successfully overcome the limitations of the conventional PINNs, delivering simulations with high fidelity.
